The Journey of Empathy

1. Adopt

Purchase a physical NFC-enabled card representing a specific endangered species. 100% of profits go to global wildlife sanctuaries.

2. Scan

Tap the premium metal/bamboo card to your smartphone. Our app instantly authenticates your unique adoption serial number.

3. Protect

Experience the animal in stunning Augmented Reality right in your room. Track their real-world conservation status and learn how to help.

Impact & Vision

We envision a world where technology creates empathy, and empathy drives action.

Every scan is a connection. Every adoption is a step toward saving their future. By bridging the digital and physical worlds, we are giving a voice to those who cannot speak for themselves.
